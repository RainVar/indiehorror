## Description

**The house hears you.**

Mama only asked you to bring home the papers. But Lola left six instructions, and the old house doesn't want you to forget a single one.

*Bulong sa Bahay* is a short, first-person folklore horror story set in a fictional Southern Luzon home. Close the capiz windows. Turn the family portrait away. Stop the clock. And when the house starts listening—be careful what you give it.

- Explore fourteen rooms over two storeys, a courtyard and a cramped crawlspace, with lightweight PSX-inspired visuals.
- Survive a creature that follows noise; crouch, hide, or lure it with your phone.
- Optional microphone mode lets your own reactions become part of the game. The full game works without a microphone.
- Commit early to two different routes: ward the house, or return a forgotten name and escape.
- Discover three family memories, varied encounters and two first-person ending cutscenes.
- Play in Filipino or English, with adjustable graphics and sound captions.

Headphones recommended. Browser controls support keyboard/mouse and landscape touchscreens. A Windows download is also available.

**Content notes:** Grief, death, supernatural pursuit and loud scares. No graphic gore. The entity and ritual are fictional.

**Microphone privacy:** Loudness is processed on your device and discarded. No recording, transcription or upload. Permission is optional and may be unavailable inside some browser embeds.

This is an initial playtest build. Please leave feedback about pacing, unclear objectives, scares, and your device's performance. Playtime varies and will be established through first-time playtests.
