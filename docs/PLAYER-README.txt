BULONG SA BAHAY — v0.2.0
The house hears you.

Run BulongSaBahay.exe. No installation or account is required.
The Windows build is unsigned; this initial build is for playtesting.

WASD / arrow keys — Move
Mouse — Look
E — Interact
Hold E / USE — Commit to an early route
Hold E / SKIP for two seconds — Skip an ending cutscene
C / Ctrl — Toggle crouch
Shift — Sprint (limited stamina)
F — Flashlight
J / Tab — Journal and instructions
Q — Throw your phone as a sound distraction; retrieve it to reuse it
Esc — Pause
F3 — Performance overlay

Microphone mode is OPTIONAL and OFF by default. The complete story and both endings
can be played without microphone access. Sound is processed on your device for its
loudness, then discarded. It is not recorded, transcribed, or uploaded.
If you enable it, use headphones and remain quiet during the three-second calibration.

Explore fourteen rooms, two floors, two staircases and a crawlspace.
Offer rice to keep Lola's promise, or play the cassette to return Tomas's name.
These are separate routes with different objectives, not a final ending selector.
Progress saves at route checkpoints, evidence milestones and each completed candle.
Being caught resets the current checkpoint. Settings and endings are also saved locally.
Unlocked cutscenes can be replayed from the ending gallery. Old saves resume at the
new early branching point; their settings, memories and unlocked endings are retained.
Browser private mode or blocked storage may prevent saves surviving a reload.

Low quality lowers render resolution and disables expensive effects. Brightness,
volume, camera motion, sound captions, and sensitivity are adjustable.
On touchscreens, use the left side to move and swipe the right side to look.
Touch buttons: E interact, C crouch, F flashlight, Q phone, J journal, II pause.

Contains grief, supernatural pursuit, loud scares, and references to death.
No graphic gore. The entity and ritual are fictional.

This is an initial playtest build. The 20–30 minute playtime is a design target,
not yet validated by independent first-time players. Native Android is planned later.
Please report platform/browser, settings, what happened, and where you were in the story.
